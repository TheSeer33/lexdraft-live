# AI Contract Review for Small Business

Small businesses often need to understand commercial agreements before signing them but do not have an in-house legal team. AI contract review can help operators identify business terms, obligations, renewal language, payment issues, intellectual-property provisions, termination rights, and clauses that deserve professional review.

LexDraft is designed as a contract-intelligence layer for founders, agencies, consultants, creators, and other small-business operators.

## What a useful AI contract review workflow should provide

- Plain-language contract summaries
- Source-grounded risk flags
- Key obligations and deadlines
- Negotiation questions
- Possible fallback positions
- Clear uncertainty statements
- Escalation points for qualified counsel

AI contract analysis should support business decision-making, not pretend to replace individualized legal advice.
