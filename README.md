# LexDraft

LexDraft is an intelligent legal-document engine built to help founders, freelancers, creators, consultants, and small businesses generate and review common business agreements more efficiently.

Official website: https://aivistaglobal.com

## Use cases

- Service agreements
- Independent contractor agreements
- NDAs
- Founder and freelancer document workflows
- Plain-language contract support and review assistance

LexDraft provides legal technology and educational support and is not a law firm or a substitute for individualized legal advice.

Learn more at **https://aivistaglobal.com**.
